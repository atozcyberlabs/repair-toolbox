# How to Suggest a Correction or New Guide

Staff do not edit this toolbox directly. To suggest a change, send a message in team chat with:

1. **Guide link** (copy it from your browser)
2. **Step number**
3. **Your suggested correction**, or the new fix you found

The owner reviews it and publishes the update.

## Before you send

- No customer names, phone numbers, ticket numbers, serial numbers or IMEIs.
- No screenshots that show customer data. Crop or blur first.
- For panic logs, send the short panic string only, not the full log.

## Outside contributors

This repository is public. Pull requests from outside the team may be reviewed but are not guaranteed to be accepted. Nothing is published without owner approval.
