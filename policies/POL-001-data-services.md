## 1. Purpose

Customers trust us with phones that hold their photos, messages, and personal information. This policy sets the rules every team member follows when we find, copy, back up, move, or recover customer data.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers every [Data Service](../data-services/) and any repair where customer data could be lost.

We only provide data services when the customer can unlock the device or sign in to their own account or backup. We do not get past passcodes or account locks, and we do not do digital forensics.

## 3. Policy statements

### 3.1 Customer consent
- **3.1.1** Ask the intake question before any work starts: "What exactly do you need back, and can you unlock the phone for us?"
- **3.1.2** Any step that could erase data (Restore, Erase, DFU, factory reset) needs the customer's separate approval, written in the ticket **before** the step.
- **3.1.3** If the only copy of the data might be on the phone, always try the data-safe option first.

### 3.2 Passcodes and accounts
- **3.2.1** The customer enters their own passcode, Apple ID, Google, and backup passwords.
- **3.2.2** Staff never ask for, write down, or store a passcode or password.
- **3.2.3** Staff never attempt to get past a passcode, account lock, or activation lock, for any reason.

### 3.3 Ownership
- **3.3.1** We work only for the device owner. If ownership is in doubt, stop and contact AJ.

### 3.4 Where data goes
- **3.4.1** Customer data goes to the customer: their account, their new phone, or their own drive.
- **3.4.2** We do not keep backups or copies for customers.
- **3.4.3** A temporary copy on a store computer is allowed only when there is no other route. It is deleted once the customer confirms their data, and no later than [AJ: set number] days after pickup. The deletion is recorded in the ticket.

### 3.5 Approved tools
- **3.5.1** Only tools listed in the repair toolbox may be used on customer devices.
- **3.5.2** A new tool is tested on a shop phone first, approved by AJ, and added to the toolbox before any customer job.

### 3.6 Verification before handoff
- **3.6.1** The customer checks the specific items they asked for (photos, contacts, files) on the destination before the job is closed.
- **3.6.2** An old phone is never erased until the customer has checked the new one and agreed.

### 3.7 Privacy at the bench
- **3.7.1** Staff look only at the data needed for the job.
- **3.7.2** No screenshots, photos, or notes of customer data. No customer details in Discord or this toolbox.

### 3.8 Records
- **3.8.1** Every data service job is tagged in RepairDesk and has a findings record in the ticket.

## 4. Exceptions

Anything not covered by this policy, or any reason to break a rule above, goes to AJ first. Only AJ can approve an exception, and it's written in the ticket.

## 5. Violations

Breaking this policy, especially sections 3.2 or 3.4, is handled under the store's staff rules and may end the person's work with AtoZ.
