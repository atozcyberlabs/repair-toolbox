**Our rule:** we protect the customer's data before we erase, replace, or give up on any device.

Board repair gets a dead phone running again. These services cover the software side: getting the phone to boot without wiping it, getting the data off once it runs, and making sure the customer never loses everything again.

> **The one rule every tech follows:** never choose **Restore**, **Erase**, or **DFU** on a customer's phone until the customer has agreed in the ticket that their data may be lost. Always try the data-safe option first.

---

## Data-first check (do this at intake, every time)

Ask these three questions before any repair starts. Write the answers in the ticket.

1. **Is the data important to you?** (photos, messages, work files)
2. **Do you have a recent backup?** (iCloud, Google, computer)
3. **Do you know your Apple ID or Google password?** (needed after any reset)

If the answers are "yes, important" and "no backup," the job is a data job first and a repair job second. Pick the protocol below.

## Data handling rules

These apply to every service in this section.

1. **The customer unlocks the phone.** Never ask for or write down a passcode.
2. **Customer data goes back to the customer.** Their iCloud, their Google account, their new phone, or their own drive.
3. **Temporary copies on store computers are deleted** within [AJ: set number] days after pickup. Record the deletion in the ticket.
4. **No customer data in this toolbox, in Discord, or in screenshots.**
5. **Tag every job in RepairDesk** with the service tag below, so we can track results.
