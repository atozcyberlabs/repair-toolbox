## Customers are keeping phones longer

The GSMA reported in 2026 that people globally keep their phones for about **3.5 years**, up from **around 2 years a decade earlier**. That's the typical time between upgrades in the report. For the bench, it means we should be ready to troubleshoot older models customers still rely on.

## Apple repair options have expanded

**Parts and calibration.** Apple's Self Service Repair program provides access to genuine parts, tools, and model-specific manuals. On iPhone 15 and later, Apple also supports reusing parts taken from another iPhone of the same model. Check Apple's compatibility list for the exact model and part, then use Repair Assistant where supported to finish calibration.

**Before you run Repair Assistant, check:**

- The phone is on the latest iOS version
- The phone is on regular Wi-Fi (not a sign-in page network, and not a work network that needs a username and password)
- The battery is above 20 percent

**California repair access.** Under California's Right to Repair Act, manufacturers must make parts, tools, and documentation available for covered products. A product is covered if it was manufactured on or after July 1, 2021, and sold or used in California on or after that date. For covered products with a wholesale price of $100 or more, that requirement lasts at least seven years after the model was last manufactured. Phones made before mid-2021 may not be covered.

## Looking ahead five to seven years

Better backup tools may handle more routine transfers. Specialist recovery will still have a role when a device fails and the customer's most important data exists only on that phone. That's our outlook for 2031 to 2033. We can test it now by tracking which cases we resolve, recover, or can't fix yet across both stores. Use the RepairDesk tags in [Data Services](../data-services/) so every job counts.

**At intake, ask what the customer can't afford to lose.** Verify what's backed up before recommending a restore or replacement. Get separate approval before any step that could erase data. See [DS-001 Software Rescue](../data-services/DS-001-software-rescue.md).
