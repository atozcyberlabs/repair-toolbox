## The short version

We want to be the shop that makes better decisions before someone erases, replaces, or gives up on a device.

Most shops sell fixes. We protect what's on the phone first, then fix it.

## Why this matters

- People keep their phones longer now, so the phone often holds years of photos and messages.
- Many customers have no recent backup. When a phone dies, the only copy of their data may be on that phone.
- A restore or reset can fix a stuck phone in minutes, and it can also erase everything. That one choice is where a shop earns or loses a customer's trust.

## What we already do well

Board repair. We handle almost all board work in-house. That brings dead phones back to life.

## What we're adding

Software services that sit on top of our repairs:

1. **Software Rescue:** fix a stuck phone without wiping it
2. **Data Rescue After Board Repair:** get the data off as soon as the board boots
3. **Backup Before Repair and Backup Setup:** protect the data before risky work, and leave every customer with a working backup
4. **New Phone Setup and Migration:** move everything to a new phone and check it with the customer

These are written up in [Data Services](../data-services/). More services are planned there too.

## What this means for you at the bench

- Ask about the data at intake, every time.
- Try the data-safe option before anything that could erase.
- Write down what you did in the ticket, and use the RepairDesk tag.
- If you're not sure, stop and ask AJ. Doing it right matters more than doing it fast.

## How we'll know it's working

We track every data service job in RepairDesk with a tag. Over the next months we'll look at how many phones we saved without wiping, how many customers left with a backup, and what customers say. Those numbers decide what we build next.
