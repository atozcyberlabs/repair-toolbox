# iPhone Guides

Pick the problem the phone is showing. Each guide helps you figure out what's actually wrong before you replace anything.

## Start here

- **New to the bench?** Read the [iPhone Repair Outlook](../briefings/iphone-repair-outlook.md) first.

- **Got wet, or you see corrosion?** Start with [Liquid Damage](diagnostics/liquid-damage.md). It's often the hidden cause behind other symptoms.
- **Won't turn on?** Start with [No Power / No Boot](diagnostics/no-power.md).
- **Turns on but won't charge?** Go to [Charging Problems](diagnostics/charging.md).
- **Turns on and charges, but drains fast or gets hot?** Go to [Battery / Overheating](diagnostics/battery-thermal.md).
- **Restarts on its own?** Go to [Random Restarts / Panic Logs](diagnostics/random-restarts/).

## Diagnostic categories

**Build order:** High guides get written first, then Medium.

| Diagnostic category | What it helps a technician determine | Build order | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| No Power / No Boot | Dead device vs. display, charging, battery, or board fault | High | [Open](diagnostics/no-power.md) |
| Random Restarts / Panic Logs | Panic indicators, restart patterns, and possible sensor faults | High | [Open](diagnostics/random-restarts/) |
| Charging Problems | Cable, port, battery, wireless charging, or board issue | High | [Open](diagnostics/charging.md) |
| Display / Touch | Display assembly vs. connector vs. logic board symptoms | High | [Open](diagnostics/display-touch.md) |
| Battery / Overheating | Battery condition, unusual drain, thermal warning, safety escalation | High | [Open](diagnostics/battery-thermal.md) |
| Liquid Damage | Whether liquid is the real cause, how far it got, and clean vs. part vs. board-level repair | High | [Open](diagnostics/liquid-damage.md) |
| Camera / Face ID / Touch ID | Module failures, functional testing, and repair limitations | Medium | Coming soon |

> **Battery safety:** routine battery swaps are handled at the bench. Stop and contact AJ only for extreme cases: a battery swollen enough to lift the screen, punctured, smoking, or too hot to hold.
