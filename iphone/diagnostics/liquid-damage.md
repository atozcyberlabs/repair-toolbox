# iPhone: Liquid Damage

**What it helps you determine:** Whether liquid is the real cause, how far it got, and whether the phone needs a clean, a part, or board-level work.

Liquid damage often shows up as another problem first: no power, charging issues, or random restarts. If you find liquid signs while working through another guide, switch to this one.

## Problem

What symptoms does this guide cover?

- Customer says the phone got wet (dropped in water, rain, spill, pool, beach)
- "Liquid Detected" alert when plugging in a cable
- Corrosion, residue, or moisture in the port or under the screen
- Mixed symptoms after water exposure: dead phone, no charging, restarts, bad speaker or mic, foggy camera

**Not covered by this guide:** phones with no liquid exposure. Use the matching diagnostic guide instead.

## Before you start

What information or equipment do you need?

**Ask the customer and write it in the ticket:**
- What liquid? Fresh water, salt water, pool water, or something sugary like soda or coffee. Salt and sugar cause corrosion faster.
- When did it happen, and how long was it wet?
- Did they try to charge it or keep turning it on afterward?
- Did they put it in rice? (It doesn't help, but it tells you how long it sat.)

**Have ready:**
- Flashlight or magnifier
- 99% isopropyl alcohol and a soft ESD-safe brush
- Lint-free swabs

**Set expectations before opening the phone:** liquid damage can cause problems that show up days later, even after a good clean. Note in the ticket that the customer understands this.

> Never ask for the passcode. The customer unlocks the device themselves.

## Steps

What should you check, and in what order?

1. **Safety check.** Look for a swollen battery (screen lifting), smoke, burning smell, or heat. If you see any of these, stop and go to "Stop and contact AJ."
2. **Don't charge it.** Do not plug in a cable while the port may be wet, and don't keep power-cycling it.
3. **Check for the "Liquid Detected" alert.** If the customer sees it when plugging in, the port is wet. Leave it unplugged.
4. **Check the liquid contact indicator (LCI)** where accessible, such as inside the SIM tray slot. Red or pink means it has been triggered. White or silver means it hasn't.
5. **Inspect the charging port** with a light. Look for moisture, green or white corrosion, or debris.
6. **Open the phone** (with customer approval) and **disconnect the battery first**.
7. **Inspect inside:** connectors, flex cables, and the board for corrosion, white or green residue, or water marks.
8. **Clean what you can reach:** connectors and port with 99% isopropyl alcohol and a soft brush. Let it dry fully before reconnecting.
9. **Reassemble and test** using the matching diagnostic guide, then run the [post-repair checklist](../quality-checks/post-repair-checklist.md).

## Results

What did you find, and which step comes next?

| If you found... | It likely means... | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| LCI not triggered, no corrosion anywhere | Liquid probably isn't the cause | Go to the guide for the main symptom: [No Power](no-power.md), [Charging](charging.md), [Random Restarts](random-restarts/) |
| LCI triggered, corrosion only in the charging port | Port-level exposure | Clean the port, dry it, then follow [Charging](charging.md) |
| Corrosion on connectors or flex cables | Liquid got inside | Clean connectors, dry fully, reconnect, and retest |
| Corrosion on the board, under chips, or no power after cleaning | Board-level damage | Stop and contact AJ |
| Swollen, hot, or smoking battery | Safety risk | Stop and contact AJ right away |

## Stop and contact AJ

When should you stop following this guide?

Stop and message AJ on Discord if:

- The battery is swollen, hot, smoking, or punctured
- Corrosion is on the logic board or under chips (board-level cleaning or repair)
- The phone still has no power after cleaning and drying
- The customer hasn't agreed to the liquid damage risk in the ticket

Include the device model, the liquid type, what you found, and which steps you already tried. No customer details in Discord.
